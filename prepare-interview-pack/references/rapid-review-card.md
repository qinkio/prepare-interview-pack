# 30-Minute Rapid-Review Card

Create this companion when the user explicitly asks for a last-minute card, the interview is within 24 hours, or the complete handbook is too long to rehearse comfortably in one sitting. It supplements the full handbook and must not delete the full answer library.

## Outcome

The candidate should be able to open one page and complete one rehearsal loop in 30 minutes without searching the full handbook. Optimize for retrieval and spoken delivery, not analytical completeness.

Aim for roughly 2,000–4,500 Chinese characters or the equivalent in the output language. Shorten repeated context before shortening ownership boundaries, results, or stop sentences.

## Required Order

1. Last-updated date, interview round assumption, and one-sentence usage rule.
2. A six-step, 30-minute rehearsal schedule.
3. One unresolved role or company identity question when it materially changes the answer strategy.
4. One-line positioning and the complete 60-second introduction.
5. Primary story memory card: four-word retrieval chain, conclusion, three actions, verified result, ownership boundary, three-layer follow-up, and stop sentence.
6. Backup story memory card with the same essentials in a shorter form.
7. The three most consequential gap answers as complete short scripts.
8. Five P0 questions with one-line answer structures.
9. Three tailored interviewer questions.
10. Five-line fact and prohibition card.
11. A link back to the complete handbook.

Do not include long company research, competency scoring tables, the full secondary question library, source paths, or a second analysis appendix. If company stage affects the interview, compress STARS to one sentence or leave it in the full handbook.

## Speaking and Memory Layers

- Put complete words intended for the candidate to say in blockquotes.
- Put retrieval chains, factual anchors, ownership boundaries, follow-ups, and prohibitions outside blockquotes.
- Preserve the same numerical claims and ownership scope as the full handbook.
- Never introduce a new claim while shortening.
- Keep the top gap answer strengths-led: acknowledge the gap, show adjacent proof, explain the learning method, and state an early action.

## Connected-Document Publication

Search for an existing card with the exact role title before creating one. Create or update the card as a child or clearly related document when the service supports that structure. Add a prominent link near the top of the full handbook. Read both artifacts back after writing and verify:

- The parent link resolves to the rapid-review card.
- The card includes every required section.
- There are no duplicated major headings.
- Neither artifact is truncated and there are no unknown blocks hiding required content when those signals are available.
- Candidate-facing speech contains no source provenance or internal claim-status language.

## Rehearsal Test

The card passes only if a candidate can answer these without opening the full handbook:

1. Who are you and why this role?
2. What is your strongest project and personal contribution?
3. What is the backup proof?
4. What are the three largest gaps and truthful responses?
5. What will you ask the interviewer?
6. Which claims must you not overstate?
