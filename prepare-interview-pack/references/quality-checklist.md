# Quality Checklist

Complete this check before delivery or publication.

## Routing

- The mode matches the deadline or records the user's override.
- The interview-round lens is confirmed or explicitly labeled as an assumption.
- Sprint mode keeps the complete core answer library while compressing analysis and research.
- Every mode includes a company-and-role STARS map with priorities, culture hypotheses, and interview implications.
- The output language follows the user.

## Evidence Integrity

- A JD and candidate evidence source were actually read.
- Every material claim has a status and source pointer.
- Every numerical result, historical ownership claim, and past business outcome in speaking scripts is Verified.
- Candidate-confirmed statements are limited to qualitative personal facts and motivations.
- Derived-safe statements are framed as methods, interpretations, or future actions rather than historical results.
- Ownership is labeled as owned, co-owned, participated, or inherited.
- Team outcomes are not presented as personal outcomes.
- Metrics retain unit, definition, timeframe, denominator, and ownership scope when relevant.
- Conflicts, inferences, and unsupported claims are separated from speakable content.

## Analysis Integrity

- The recruiter first-impression diagnostic distinguishes HR and hiring-manager lenses.
- The first-impression verdict is conditional on supplied evidence and does not pretend to know the real applicant pool.
- Company identity is resolved across recruiting entity, parent, brand or business unit, and actual team when relevant.
- Any unresolved company identity is labeled and converted into a P0 confirmation question.
- The role mission is a business problem rather than a duty paraphrase.
- Five competencies include auditable estimated scores and decisive JD evidence.
- Must-haves and preferred qualifications are separated.
- STARS evaluates company, business unit, and role separately or says Not classifiable.
- STARS and culture conclusions include confidence and evidence.
- Synthesized beliefs are not presented as real quotations.

## Answer Quality

- The Battle Card is first and independently usable under time pressure.
- The complete 60-second introduction and primary or backup story cards are not separated by long analysis.
- The 60-second introduction has positioning, relevant evidence, and motivation.
- Drafted answers fit approximate spoken-duration limits.
- Stories state exact role and key judgment early.
- Primary and backup stories cover different top competencies.
- The primary story scores at least 70 on directness, or is explicitly labeled provisional.
- Each P0 story includes three-layer follow-ups.
- Every core question has a complete spoken answer, not only bullets.
- Every P0 question includes an evidence anchor, likely follow-up, and stop sentence or safe boundary.
- The handbook contains at least ten to twelve high-probability questions.
- The 60- and 90-second introductions are both present.
- Each introduction and project story has a transition or stop sentence.
- Gap answers acknowledge the gap and show adjacent evidence plus an early action plan.
- Strengths and evidence dominate the speaking content; risk language does not dominate the narrative.
- Culture phrases connect to real projects.
- Interviewer questions are specific to current role challenges.

## Rehearsal Integrity

- The scorecard is included when required by mode.
- Candidate scores remain blank until the candidate actually responds.
- Any observed credibility or ownership score below 4 triggers revision.
- The final claim card is short enough for immediate pre-interview review.

## Publication

- Only mode-required sections are included, in standard order.
- Existing unrelated Notion content and approved facts are preserved.
- The page includes a last-updated date and no duplicated standard headings.
- The published page is fetched again and checked.
- The handoff includes the page or file link and immediate P0 priority.
- `scripts/validate_pack.py` completed successfully for the selected mode, or any inability to run it was disclosed.
