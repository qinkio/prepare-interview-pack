# Read and maintain the current career vault directly

The local career vault is the single maintained source. Do not require or prefer a separately maintained JSON bundle for ordinary resume writing or interview preparation.

## Read for a task

Locate the user's vault from the workspace. Follow its current homepage and layout mapping; logical English table names refer to roles and may live under Chinese names in subfolders. Never require root-level copies or hidden compatibility links. Read its current homepage, career timeline/profile and project directory, then the complete relevant project cards and current canonical-claims.csv. For interviews cover every project on the actually submitted resume. Include relevant business, merchant, AI and earlier career evidence, not just one preselected subset. Use sources.csv and review-queue.csv when provenance or a substantive unresolved issue affects the exact sentence.

Read the complete project account: background, problem, candidate responsibility, decisions, actions, difficulties and results. Keep confirmed improvement figures in the results. Missing optional exact months or finer measurement notes belong in refinements, not a blanket downgrade of confirmed content. Unresolved comparison definitions or attribution that would make a sentence misleading require qualified wording or a separate refinement. Preserve the distinction between team results, personal actions and planned functionality.

Current truth status and publication permission live in canonical-claims.csv. Follow claim-aliases.csv when an old ID has been consolidated; never count old and current expressions as separate achievements. Archived records, historical compatibility tables and former exports are not the current source. Project narrative supplies full context; copied status tables do not override the current master table.

## Maintain once

When the user requests a vault update, integrate explicit candidate facts into the relevant existing project or career record and its atomic claims. Newly extracted unconfirmed facts remain proposed, but still retain the useful content and figures. Keep missing details in the project’s refinement section. Preserve stable IDs and originals, log substantive changes, and apply existing snapshot requirements for bulk changes. Do not overwrite unrelated work.

Application or interview work does not silently change career facts. If it reveals new candidate information, route it to the vault workflow within the user's authorized scope. No recurring export regeneration is required: the next task reads the current vault.

## Read permission and publication are separate

Direct local reading does not authorize uploading the entire vault. Respect source access permissions; external wording still requires adequate confirmed facts and permission for the intended purpose. Use only necessary scoped information in a resume, interview script or external artifact. A one-time scoped export is optional for an actual transfer or connector limitation, not the long-term source or a prerequisite for local skills.
